Legal Provisions & Individual Donation Limits

Key structural provisions from Section 88 of the Electoral Act 2022:

  • Section 88(8): No individual or other entity shall donate to a candidate more than ₦50,000,000 (2022) / ₦500,000,000 (2026).
  • Section 89: Political parties must submit audit reports of campaign expenses within 6 months after an election.
  • Penalties: Candidates breaching these sections are liable to fines of up to ₦1,000,000 or imprisonment.

PoliTrack Audit Methodology

A transparent guide explaining how PoliTrack estimates, calculates, and defends campaign expenditure logs.

1. Core Calculation Formulas

Political rally costs are computed programmatically using direct market parameters. The total cost of any rally event is the sum of four main logistics areas:

  • Transportation & Logistics (Buses): Buses Hired × Bus Hire Cost per Day (Covers vehicles mobilized to convey delegates from local governments).
  • Convoy Fuel Expenses: (Buses + SUVs) × Fuel Liters × Price per Liter (Estimates the cost of fueling convoy vehicles and transport buses).
  • Mobilization Allowances: Number of Attendees × Allowance per Attendee (Stipends paid to delegates and attendees to cover local transportation and feeding).
  • Operations & Media: Venue Hire + Publicity Cost (Covers renting stadiums/halls, stage setups, sound systems, billboards, and radio/TV coverage).

2. Defensible Market Baseline Rates

To ensure estimates are defensible in public discourse, PoliTrack uses conservative baseline rates compiled from local market surveys in Nigeria:

Parameter Standard Baseline Rate Market Rationale / Defense Source
Bus Hire (Coaster/HiAce) ₦60,000 / day Average commercial charter rate in major states (Lagos, Abuja, Rivers).
Convoy SUV Fuel 50 Liters / vehicle Conservative consumption estimate for inter-city travel and idling.
Fuel Price ₦1,200 / Liter Reflects current average PMS prices at filling stations.
Transport Allowance ₦10,000 / person Standard stipend distributed by campaigns to cover logistics.
Stadium/Venue Hire ₦5,000,000 Standard government rental fees for major city stadiums.
Campaign Publicity ₦15,000,000 Estimates billboards, radio jingles, and television ads.

3. AI Semantic Parsing (OpenAI Integration)

When you paste an article URL or run the Auto-Discovery tool, the system crawls the text and asks OpenAI to identify quantities (e.g. "thousands of supporters", "120 buses"). If specific numbers are missing, the AI applies conservative fallbacks based on descriptions (e.g., matching a "national stadium" crowd description to standard stadium capacities).
